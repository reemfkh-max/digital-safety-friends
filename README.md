# 🛡️ Digital Safety Friends

## 📌 About the Project

**Digital Safety Friends** is my Cybersecurity graduation project, developed as a website focused on protecting children from the risks of cyberspace and promoting cybersecurity awareness.

The project aims to help children better understand online risks and encourage safer behavior while using the internet and digital technologies.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Our goal is to contribute to a safer and more sustainable cyberspace by raising cybersecurity awareness among children and emphasizing the importance of digital safety from an early age.

## 🔐 Focus Areas

* Cybersecurity Awareness
* Child Online Safety
* Digital Safety Education
* Cyber Threat Awareness
* Safe Internet Practices

## 💡 Why This Project?

Children are an important part of our digital future. As their use of technology increases, cybersecurity awareness becomes increasingly important.

Digital Safety Friends was created to make digital safety awareness more accessible and relevant to younger users.
